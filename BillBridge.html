How it works

Three simple steps. Everyone keeps their dignity.

📝

1. Post your bill

Add the biller, amount, due date and a short note. You can stay anonymous.

🤝

2. Someone helps

Supporters browse open bills and pay the biller directly using the reference number.

✅

3. Confirm & close

Mark it paid once the biller confirms. Transparent progress for everyone.
